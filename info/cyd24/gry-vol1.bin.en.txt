A volume of six games for K-OS, in Polish and English, portrait screen, with the Chip-K mascot. You pick games from tiles in the volume menu.
Status: the games are tested on a computer; the store description has no record of this version being tested on a board, and the frame rate on the board has not been measured.
Games: 2048; Lunar Lander (30 levels, gravity, wind, fuel); Overload (an endless runner: tap - jump, hold - slide); Punch-Through (Arkanoid style); Payload (Tetris style, with a bonus for a continuous circuit); Longshot (how far will it fly, with an upgrade workshop and lasting progress).
Needs: nothing but the board - no network. Scores, progress and saved games on the card in /gry/ (the games run without a card). Theme and language from K-OS.
Limits: in Lander the levels from 15 up are very hard or impossible. Overload and Longshot have no saved games. The full tile look needs K-OS 0.7.1 or newer.
