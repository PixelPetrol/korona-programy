The first volume of games for K-OS, in Polish and English, portrait orientation. Two games: 2048 in the K-OS version and LUNAR LANDER with 30 levels named after real places on the Moon and further out in the solar system - flight physics with gravity, wind, rotation and fuel, a main engine and two RCS thrusters under your finger, landing conditions as in the original (vertical speed, horizontal speed and angle). The game engine draws into a 4-bit buffer covering the ARENA ONLY, so the status bar and the touch zone cost no frames; touch is read straight from the XPT2046, because getTouch from TFT_eSPI takes as long as a whole frame. Scores and progress on the card in /gry/. Theme and language from the K-OS settings. EARLY VERSION: the volume menu is a plain list for now - the intended tile look and the mascot await a decision. Levels from 15 up are very hard or impossible with the original wind table (at a landing angle of +-0.15 rad the lander balances only part of the drift, so the whole touchdown has to fit in a window shorter than 2 seconds) - there is an LL_WIND_SCALE knob in the code to soften it, off by default, because the level table stays as it was designed. Checked by host tests: 2.19 million checks, zero errors, including solvability of all 30 levels by search and 360 runs of random button mashing. NOT TESTED ON HARDWARE - the frames per second figure is still a model, not a measurement. 0.1.1: the Chip-K mascot from the author's sheets instead of a generated one, a start screen with a transformation animation (breathing, golden aura, super state) - it doubles as a check of the sprites on a real panel, and FIXED DIGIT LEGIBILITY in 2048: the digit colour is chosen by real WCAG contrast instead of a brightness threshold, plus a shadow under the text; the worst contrast on the board raised from 1.06 to 3.09. 0.2.0: A THIRD GAME - OVERLOAD, an endless runner. Chip-K runs along the traces of the board, jumps over gaps in the copper and lies down under plumes of hot air; collected charge sets off the transformation and for a dozen or so seconds you run as a Saiyan - faster and untouchable. Controls: a tap is a jump, a hold is a slide; there is deliberately NO double jump. The arena is 240x140 instead of the full screen, because horizontal scrolling changes every pixel in every frame. 0.2.1: the threshold that tells a tap from a hold raised from 132 ms to 264 ms - at 132 ms a natural finger tap was caught as a slide instead of a jump. ... 0.9.2: MENU ...
