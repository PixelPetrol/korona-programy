An office suite for your fingertip, in Polish and English: notes, calculator, calendar, file manager, QR codes, exchange rates.
Status: BETA - this version has not been run on a board yet. An earlier one was tested on 2.4": start, theme and language from K-OS, calibration, RST return; the modules were not tried by finger.
Features: notes you can also type on a computer keyboard (address and QR code on screen, you type in a browser), a calculator with a tape, a calendar with an NTP clock and day notes, a two-panel file manager (copy, move, delete), QR codes (also WiFi for guests), stopwatch and timer, unit converter, NBP exchange rates (offline from the card).
Needs: an SD card (data in /office/); WiFi for rates, the clock and typing from a computer. Theme, language, networks, time zone and calibration come from K-OS.
Limits: /korona is read-only; it does not delete .bin programs in /programy. Rates and the notes page use plain http.
