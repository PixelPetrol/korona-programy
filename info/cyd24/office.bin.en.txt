An office suite for a fingertip, in Polish and English. The notepad can also be typed FROM A COMPUTER KEYBOARD: while that screen is open Office raises a small server and shows its address and QR code, so you write long text in a browser and it lands on the card. Also: a calculator with tape and operator precedence, a calendar with an NTP clock and day notes, a TWO-PANEL file manager in the spirit of Total Commander (panels stacked, because the screen is narrow: multi-select, copy with progress bar and abort, moving, deleting a tree after counting it; /korona is read-only, deleting .bin from /programy is refused, so no orphans are left after snapshots), QR codes (WIFI too: for guests), a stopwatch with timer, a unit and number converter and NBP rates over plain HTTP (no TLS, works offline from the card). Theme and language come from K-OS settings; it asks for neither the network password nor touch calibration. Data in /office/ on the card. Tested on hardware: start, data migration from the old name, theme and language from K-OS, calibration from the card, return to K-OS after RST; the modules not yet tried with a finger. 1.2.0: WRITING THAT DOES NOT LOSE DATA - five places wrote to the card without checking the result, or deleted a file's only surviving copy (worst: the notepad said 'saved' on a full card and the note was nowhere); all rewritten to one pattern: a work file, every write and fclose checked, swapped in through .bak, and on failure the old content comes back and nothing is deleted. The pattern never touches a file that is not its own (.part and .bak are legal FAT names), and after a power loss halfway through the swap it puts the file back at the next start and says so on the screen. Plus an RGB LED (four signals: timer alarm, copy finished, write error, notes server running), an NTP clock in the calendar (until now it did not sync at all unless you first opened the rates or the notes over www), a shorter network wait (6 s instead of 9 s, touch aborts) and honest messages when K-OS does not share the network with programs. 1.1.1 (only the number in the binary, code as in 1.1.0) went into 1.2.0, not released separately. 1.3.1: SEVERAL WiFi NETWORKS. K-OS can now remember up to six networks, and Office takes them all from the card and - this is the whole difference you feel - SCANS FIRST, then tries only the remembered networks that are really visible, strongest first. Until now every invisible network cost a full 12 seconds of waiting. ...
