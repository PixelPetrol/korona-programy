The same aircraft radar as SkyCYD portrait, in landscape 320x240 - the original SkyCYD layout.
Status: the portal fixes in this version (handing out addresses, the AP name) have not been tested on a board yet.
Features: aircraft positions from adsb.lol (through the SkyCYD Worker), a map of the area, aircraft photos, weather.
Needs: WiFi with internet. On the first start the board opens its own access point with a setup portal. The SD card is not used. Touch calibration is separate from the portrait version.
Limits: the interface is in Polish only. SkyCYD predates K-OS and does not read its settings from the card (theme, language, touch calibration) - it has its own, set in its portal.
