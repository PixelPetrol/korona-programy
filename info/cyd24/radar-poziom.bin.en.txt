The same aircraft radar as SkyCYD portrait, in Polish and English, in landscape 320x240 - the original SkyCYD layout.
Status: BETA - version 4.5.0 (English language) has not been run on a board yet. The portal fixes from 4.4.2 (handing out addresses, the AP name) are also waiting for a test.
Features: aircraft positions from adsb.lol (through the SkyCYD Worker), a map of the area, aircraft photos, weather.
Needs: WiFi with internet. On the first start the board opens its own access point with a setup portal. The SD card is not used. Touch calibration is separate from the portrait version.
Limits: you pick the language (Polish or English, Polish by default) in the radar menu (Jezyk / Language) or in its portal (Look tab). SkyCYD predates K-OS and does not read its settings from the card (theme, language, touch calibration) - it has its own, set in its portal.
