The same weather station as Meteo K-OS portrait, but in LANDSCAPE orientation 320x240. Here the radar has a square grid at the left edge and a side panel with the strongest rain cell, a legend and the age of the frame (in red when the frame is more than 10 minutes old). The console shows a weather icon and three detail fields. WARNING: touch calibration in TFT_eSPI is valid only for the rotation it was made in, so on the first run the program proposes a calibration recomputed from the K-OS settings and shows a check screen with a cross - you have to touch it. If it does not hit, it goes on to a full four-corner calibration and saves the result separately, in /meteo/dotyk-poziom.txt and in the K-OS snapshot. It does not overwrite the system calibration file. NOT TESTED ON HARDWARE - built from the same source as the portrait version, which was tested. 0.2.1: SEVERAL WiFi NETWORKS - the program takes the whole list of remembered networks from the card (K-OS now remembers up to six of them) and SCANS FIRST, so as to try only those that are visible, strongest first. Until now every invisible network cost a full 12 seconds of waiting at start. Networks with a hidden SSID are tried last, so they still work.
