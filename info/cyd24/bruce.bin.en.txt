Bruce - a multi-protocol 'pentest toolkit' (WiFi, BLE, IR, RF), the LITE variant. Env KORONA_CYD24: corrected colours (INVON). TOUCH: the calibration is separate for each screen orientation - the first start asks for four corners in the landscape layout, and after changing Config > Orientation Bruce asks for them once for the new layout and then remembers it (K-OS keeps this in a snapshot). Without that, touch in portrait did not hit anything at all: TFT_eSPI computes touch from the calibration bounds, but with the dimensions of the current rotation, so a calibration from landscape gave a stretch in portrait instead of a rotation.
