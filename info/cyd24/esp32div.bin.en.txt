ESP32-DIV in its HaleHound-CYD variant: a WiFi and BLE scanner, deauth, RF and NFC tools (optional modules). A third-party program, ported for the CYD 2.4".
Status: tested on a board on 03.09.2026: start, menu, touch, RST returns to K-OS. The individual tools are not described as tested.
Needs: nothing but the board; you add RF/NFC modules yourself.
Limits: use it only on your own networks and devices.
Authors: cifertech / Wontfallo (HaleHound-CYD). In the K-OS port touch is read over the screen bus (the previous way froze the start screen).
