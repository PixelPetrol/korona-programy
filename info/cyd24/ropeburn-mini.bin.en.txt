A rhythm skill game, portrait screen: the rope turns faster and faster and YOU jump - every touch of the screen is a jump that must land in the window as the rope reaches the feet. No touch, or a touch outside the window, is a stumble: the heroine trips and loses one of THREE LIVES while the rope keeps turning; the third stumble is a collapse and the end of the run. Hitting the exact centre of the window pays x5. A calm start (57 turns per minute), 151/min after 100 jumps - a good run lasts 70-90 s. The multiplier doubles every 20 jumps (up to x32); every threshold is a step of the heroine's fatigue, a step of dusk over the stage and a reward picture. The stage is the author's pixel-art backyard, and defeat has its own illustration. A metronome on the buzzer raises its pitch with each threshold. Records: two tables per level (clean run and head start), 5 places with initials. Unlocks with no shop and no currency: the next level for threshold 3, extra lives (up to 5) for 10 completed runs and for threshold 4. State lives in NVS (survives removing the card); the card holds only a human-readable copy in /ropeburn/. 8-bit graphics, 64 colours per character, all in flash, no network.
