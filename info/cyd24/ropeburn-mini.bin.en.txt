A rhythm skill game, portrait screen: the rope turns faster and faster and you jump - every touch of the screen is a jump that must land in the window.
Status: BETA, UNTESTED - this version has not been run on a board yet, only tested on a computer.
Features: three lives, x5 for hitting the exact centre of the window, a multiplier that grows every 20 jumps up to x32, upgrade cards during the run (you pick with a touch, the game never pauses), a metronome on the buzzer, records with initials. A good run lasts 70-90 s. The game itself unlocks further levels and extra lives, with no shop and no currency.
Needs: nothing but the board - no network. Progress is kept in the board memory; on the SD card (if present) it puts a readable copy in /ropeburn/.
Limits: 1 character of 5 and 2 levels of 5 are ready (Backyard and Wind).
