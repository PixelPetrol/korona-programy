A rhythm skill game, portrait screen: the heroine skips rope ON HER OWN and the player has one job - touch the screen at the right moment, which performs a DOUBLE JUMP for big points. A missed touch is a stumble: she trips and loses one of THREE LIVES while the rope keeps turning; the third stumble is a collapse and the end of the run. Touch nothing and nothing happens - the whole tension is risk for points. The multiplier doubles every 20 jumps (up to x32), and every threshold is a step of the heroine's fatigue (blush, sweat, hair) and a step of dusk in the sky - you see how far you got without reading the counter. A metronome on the buzzer raises its pitch with each threshold. Records: two tables per level (clean run and head start), 5 places with initials. Unlocks with no shop and no currency: the next level for reaching threshold 3, extra lives (up to 5) for 10 completed runs and for threshold 4. State lives in NVS (survives removing the card); the card holds only a human-readable copy in /ropeburn/. 8-bit graphics, 64 colours per character, all in flash - the game uses no network. READY: 1 character of 5, 2 levels of 5 (Backyard and Wind - gusts change the tempo). EARLY VERSION, NOT TESTED ON HARDWARE: 228 tests on the computer, zero runs on a board.
