A weather station for a fingertip, in Polish and English, in PORTRAIT orientation. Four views switched in the menu (by default Console, then Tiles, Rain radar and Clock) - the choice comes back after a restart. Weather from Open-Meteo in a single CSV request over plain HTTP, without a key: current conditions, 5 days, 24 hours, a nowcast every quarter of an hour, sunrise and sunset and the moon phase. The rain radar is an IMGW-PIB 64x64 grid with a range of 119 km, taken from the same server as the photos in SkyCYD - under the rainfall it draws rivers, borders and the names of nearby towns from a vector file on the card (Natural Earth, public domain, and GeoNames under CC BY 4.0 - attribution on the 'about' screen). The town is entered with the on-screen keyboard, and names from the network are transliterated, because the fonts have ASCII only. It takes the theme and language from the K-OS settings, or you can pick one of three of its own (Burza, Swit, Neon). It works without a network - it shows the last data from the card with its date. The battery indicator appears ONLY when somebody solders a voltage divider to GPIO35 themselves and the reading is trustworthy; the divider only MEASURES, this program does not solve powering the board from a cell. Data in /meteo/ on the card. TESTED ON HARDWARE 04.09.2026: start, theme and language from K-OS, calibration from the card, connecting to the network, fetching the weather (5 blocks) and an IMGW radar frame, saving for offline mode, return to K-OS after RST. 0.1.1 fixes a truncated request address (a 560 character buffer for an address 589 long - the server returned HTTP 400 and there was no weather at all). THE MAP FILE /meteo/mapa.bin has to be copied onto the card separately - without it the radar works, but without rivers and names. 0.2.1: SEVERAL WiFi NETWORKS - the program takes the whole list of remembered networks from the card (K-OS now remembers up to six of them) and SCANS FIRST, so as to try only those that are visible, strongest first. Until now every invisible network cost a full 12 seconds of waiting at start. Networks with a hidden SSID are tried last, so they still work.
