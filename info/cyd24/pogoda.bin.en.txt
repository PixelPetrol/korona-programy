A weather station on LVGL: a big temperature, an icon, a 3-day forecast, an hourly chart, themes. Data from Open-Meteo without an API key - location by IP or coordinates. First start: AP 'cyd-weather-station-XXXX' (open), portal 192.168.4.1 (WiFi, location, time zone); then http://cyd-weather-station.local/. After 'save and restart' the board goes back to K-OS and autostart resumes the application.
