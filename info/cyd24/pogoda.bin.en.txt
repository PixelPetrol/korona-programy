A weather station on LVGL: a big temperature, a weather icon, a 3-day forecast, an hourly chart, themes. A third-party program.
Status: UNTESTED on a board.
Needs: WiFi. Data from Open-Meteo without an API key, location by IP or from coordinates. First start: an open access point 'cyd-weather-station-XXXX', portal 192.168.4.1 (WiFi, location, time zone); then the page http://cyd-weather-station.local/.
Limits: after 'save and restart' the board goes back to K-OS and autostart resumes the application.
Author: nicholaswilde. Image prepared for K-OS: RST returns to the menu.
