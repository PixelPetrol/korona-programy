ESP32 Marauder - WiFi and Bluetooth LE auditing: scanning networks and BLE devices, sniffing, testing your own network. A third-party program.
Status: UNTESTED - the image has only been compiled; the touch calibration still needs a finger to confirm it.
Board: CYD 2.8" (2432S028R) with the ILI9341 panel.
Needs: nothing but the board. Settings in SPIFFS - thanks to the K-OS snapshots they survive between starts.
Limits: use it only on your own networks and devices.
Authors: justcallmekoko / Fr4nkFletcher. Image prepared for K-OS: RST returns to the menu.
