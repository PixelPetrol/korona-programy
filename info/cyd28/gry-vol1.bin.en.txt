A volume of six games for K-OS, in Polish and English, portrait screen, with the Chip-K mascot.
Status: UNTESTED - nobody has reported running this binary on 2.8" yet.
Board: CYD 2.8" (2432S028R).
Games: 2048, Lunar Lander, Overload (a runner), Punch-Through (Arkanoid style), Payload (Tetris style), Longshot (how far will it fly). The same as on 2.4".
Needs: nothing but the board - no network. Scores and progress on the card in /gry/. Touch calibration comes from K-OS; when the card has none for this board, it says so at start instead of guessing. Theme and language from K-OS.
Limits: the full tile look needs K-OS 0.7.1 or newer.
