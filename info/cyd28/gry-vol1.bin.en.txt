The same volume of games as on the 2.4", built for the CYD 2.8" (2432S028R): touch bit-banged on the board's own pins, backlight GPIO 21. The games have no calibration of their own - they take it from K-OS; when there is none on the card for this board, the volume says so outright at start instead of using guessed numbers. NOT TESTED ON HARDWARE.
