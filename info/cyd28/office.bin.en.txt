The same Office as on the 2.4", built for the CYD 2.8" (2432S028R). On this board the XPT2046 touch sits on ITS OWN pins (CLK 25, MOSI 32, MISO 39, CS 33, IRQ 36) and not on the screen bus, so it is read by bit-banging - the same solution that has long worked in K-OS. Backlight on GPIO 21 instead of 27. IMPORTANT: the default touch calibration for the 2.8" is GUESSED and the program knows it - on the first start without the file /korona/cyd28/dotyk.txt it shows a full-screen warning and offers a four-corner calibration instead of quietly using bad numbers. That screen has no buttons on purpose, because with a bad calibration a button may be impossible to hit - any touch read raw from the converter counts. NOT TESTED ON HARDWARE.
