An office suite for your fingertip, in Polish and English: notes, calculator, calendar, file manager, QR codes, exchange rates.
Status: BETA, UNTESTED - it has not been run on 2.8" yet.
Board: CYD 2.8" (2432S028R).
Features: the same as on 2.4" - notes typed from a browser too, a calculator with a tape, a calendar with an NTP clock, a two-panel file manager, QR codes, stopwatch and timer, unit converter, NBP exchange rates.
Needs: an SD card (data in /office/); WiFi for rates, the clock and typing from a computer. Theme, language, networks, time zone and calibration come from K-OS. Without the file /korona/cyd28/dotyk.txt it shows a warning and offers a four-corner calibration - any touch counts.
Limits: /korona is read-only; it does not delete .bin programs in /programy. Rates and the notes page use plain http.
