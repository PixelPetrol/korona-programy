The same Office as on the 2.8", rebuilt for the revision with the ST7789 screen driver (the board with TWO USB sockets, sometimes described as v3). Touch, pins and the GPIO 21 backlight are THE SAME as in the ILI9341 version - the only difference is the screen driver, which cannot be chosen at run time because it is compiled into the library. That is why this board has a separate identifier (cyd28s) and separate images. NOT TESTED ON HARDWARE, AND IT CANNOT BE TESTED WITHOUT THAT BOARD. Two things are visible a second after the first start and both are fixable with one flag, without touching the code: the whole image as a NEGATIVE and RED SWAPPED WITH BLUE. If it comes out that way - write in, the fix is one rebuilt image. The touch calibration lies in /korona/cyd28s/dotyk.txt and has to be done once separately, even though the touch panel is the same as in cyd28.
