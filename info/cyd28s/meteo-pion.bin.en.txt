A weather station for your fingertip, in Polish and English, portrait screen: an Open-Meteo forecast and the IMGW rain radar.
Status: BETA, UNTESTED - it has not been run on this revision yet. If the picture comes out as a negative or with red and blue swapped, tell us - it is a one-flag fix.
Board: CYD 2.8" ST7789 (two USB sockets).
Features: the same as on 2.4" - four views, current conditions, 5 days, 24 hours, a quarter-hourly nowcast, the IMGW radar with a map, offline mode from the card.
Needs: WiFi (Open-Meteo, no key), an SD card (data in /meteo/; K-OS 0.7.8 or newer fetches the map /meteo/mapa.bin itself, with an older one copy it from the store website). Networks, language, time zone and calibration come from K-OS.
Map: Natural Earth (public domain), GeoNames (CC BY 4.0).
