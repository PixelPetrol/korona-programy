A version for the CYD 2.8" with an ST7789 panel (the board with two USB sockets) - built, NOT TESTED ON HARDWARE. On this revision the colours are decided by TWO switches, not one: the ST7789 driver in the library sends INVON unconditionally at start, and the radar itself sets the inversion its own way after initialisation. The profile sets both so that the result comes out in line with the canonical setting of this board. Should the image still come out as a negative - it can be switched DIRECTLY IN THE RADAR MENU (settings -> inversion), without rebuilding anything. Landscape orientation 320x240; touch calibration separate from the portrait version. The SkyCYD interface is in Polish only.
