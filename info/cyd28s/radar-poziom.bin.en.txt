The same aircraft radar as SkyCYD portrait, in landscape 320x240 (the original SkyCYD layout).
Status: UNTESTED - nobody has run SkyCYD on this revision yet. If the picture comes out as a negative, you switch it in the radar menu (settings -> inversion).
Board: CYD 2.8" ST7789 (two USB sockets).
Features: aircraft positions from adsb.lol (through the SkyCYD Worker), a map of the area, aircraft photos, weather.
Needs: WiFi with internet. On the first start the board opens its own access point with a setup portal. The 2.8" touch calibration is in the portal (Network -> Touch calibration), separate from the portrait version. The SD card is not used.
Limits: the interface is in Polish only. SkyCYD predates K-OS and does not read its settings from the card (theme, language, touch calibration).
