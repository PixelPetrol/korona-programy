The same volume of games as on the 2.8", rebuilt for the revision with an ST7789 panel (the board with two USB sockets). Touch, pins and the GPIO 21 backlight are the same as in the ILI9341 version. The games have no calibration of their own - they take it from K-OS; when there is none on the card for this board, the volume says so outright at start instead of using guessed numbers (file /korona/cyd28s/dotyk.txt). NOT TESTED ON HARDWARE.
