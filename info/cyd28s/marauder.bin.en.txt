Marauder for the CYD 2.8" in the 2 USB revision, with an ST7789 panel. Hardware-identical to the cyd28 version - the same screen pins, the same touch on a separate bus, backlight GPIO 21 - it differs only in the display driver, the colour order and the inversion setting. Return to K-OS after RST is assured, settings in SPIFFS. NOT TESTED ON HARDWARE. To look at on the board: the colours, the image orientation and the layout of the status bar - Marauder for the ST7789 uses coordinates tuned for a bigger screen, so small text shifts are possible.
