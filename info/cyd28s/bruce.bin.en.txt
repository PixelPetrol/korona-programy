Bruce - a security testing toolkit (WiFi, BLE, IR, RF), LITE variant. A third-party program.
Status: UNTESTED - the screen settings are derived from the library code, not from a test on a board. Possible faults: colours (negative, red and blue swapped) or a picture rotated by 180 degrees - then touch lands in mirrored places.
Board: CYD 2.8" ST7789 (two USB sockets).
Needs: nothing but the board; you add IR and RF modules yourself.
Limits: use it only on your own networks and devices.
Author: pr3y (the Bruce project). Image prepared for K-OS: RST returns to the menu.
